About Our Practice

I am Lonni Gallagher...

As a Colon Hydrotherapist and Licensed Massage Therapist, Lonni Gallagher practices with the intent to provide support for whole body wellness through natural restorative care. She received her colon hydrotherapy certification from the Cathy Shea International School of Colon Hydrotherapy in Juno Beach, Florida in 2022, and earned her Associate of Occupational Studies (AOS) degree in Massage Therapy from Mildred Elley in Albany, New York in 2026.

Her expertise lies in helping individuals achieve optimal intestinal health, relieve digestive discomfort, and enhance their overall well-being through the practice of colon hydrotherapy. With a focus on patient education and empowerment, Lonni strives to create a positive and transformative experience for each client. She firmly believes that a healthy colon is a cornerstone of overall wellness and vitality, and she is committed to guiding her clients on their journey toward achieving and maintaining optimal gut health.

Lonni believes whole body wellness starts from the inside out. By creating a healthy happy gut and focusing on relaxation Lonni's clients are supported in reaching their goals of feeling balanced, refreshed, and revitalized. Combining therapeutic bodywork and colon hydrotherapy, Lonni is passionate about providing clients with the opportunity to focus on healing, destressing, and improving their quality of life while in a safe comfortable supportive environment. Whether you are seeking digestive help, deep relaxation or release of muscle tension, Lonni wants you to walk away feeling your best on the inside and out!

Our Wellness Philosophy

Gallagher Wellness Center believes that wellness care plays an essential role in supporting the body’s natural ability to heal itself. Treatment approaches are designed to improve spinal alignment, reduce stress on the nervous system, and enhance overall physical performance.

Every patient receives careful evaluation and a customized plan tailored to their lifestyle, health history, and recovery goals.

Patient‑Centered Care Approach

Patients are encouraged to actively participate in their treatment process through posture awareness, movement education, and targeted home exercises that support long‑term improvement.

By combining chiropractic adjustments, therapeutic exercise, and wellness guidance, the clinic helps patients maintain lasting results beyond the treatment room.
